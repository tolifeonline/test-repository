METHOD

Earth's three-dimensional heliocentric position is compared with its position on January 1.

The resulting orbital angle represents progress through the yearly orbital journey.

UNDERSTAND THE CLOCK

January 1 begins at 12 o'clock. The orbital journey progresses around the visual clock.
